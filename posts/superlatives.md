title: Superlatives are literally and objectively the worst thing ever
---

- Belgian culture: _ça va_, glorified mediocrity, which is awfully underrated
- tricked ourselves into thinking that we can only enjoy the best. If we enjoyed it, we must sell it as the best.
- showoff culture by giving everyone a megaphone. We only hear about the best of the best, the highlights.
- Americanisation causes the downfall of the introvert
- _amazing_ means _good_, _good_ means _fine_, _fine_ and _alright_ mean _bad_. We cannot have _knowledge_, only _expertise_. No _interest_, only _passion_ and _excitement_. Businesses do not offer _services_, they offer _solutions_. We cannot have _contributors_, only _heroes_.
- countless personal experiences have taught me that a lack of superlatives is perceived as a sign of soullessness, indifference, and lethargy.

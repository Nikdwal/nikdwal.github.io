## Introduction
I have always considered myself a rationalist for as long as I knew the word. I am a lover of mathematics and precise reasoning. I have also considered myself a creative type and artist for at least as long as that. To some, that is a suprising combination, but it is hardly strange to me at all.
I see it as exploring multiple facets of what it means to be human: the ability to reason and the ability to create abstract impressions. I am intrigued by the sheer breadth of experience the human mind is capable of. While pondering this breadth, I have found a link between rationality, impressionism, verbal, and non-verbal communication. I present a system of purely hypothetical value, a formalised version of which will undoubtedly crumble under scrutiny. 

## Clarity of thought

- clarity of thought is a depressingly underrated virtue
- rationality is profoundly useful in communication as it structures our thoughts before we communicate them in a format that will be easier to unpack
   - even theorised that reasoning ability evolved as a social tool
   - insofar as emotional intelligence has a precise definition, it must involve the ability to reason to be worthy of its name, and I believe that it is
   - surprising reacions when I say rationality is high up my value hierarchy when interacting with others - all I want is to follow their thoughts and have them understand mine
- communication entails sending a message that you know will be received as intended
- rationalists use precise language, as language is the only vehicle that can be made precise enough for this purpose

## Impressionism

## Entanglement of dimensions
- we have two dimensions: precision of the ideas we want to communicate and tools we have available
- crucially, it feels to me like every tool really only excels at one job, hence the dimensions are not really separate.
  The rationalist's tools are words and numbers. The impressionist's tools are paintbrushes and guitar strings.
- assuming communication is about conveying information to others, consider how attached you are to the message being received as intended
  - if creativity and divergence matter, use impressionistic means
  - if you want the other person to see clearly what you think, use rationalist means
- You could use a wrench to smash a nail into a board, but why would you?
  
- To rely on the suboptimal tool for either rationality or impressionism would undersell the sheer lengths we can go to as people.
- There was no tool I could have used to lay out my thougts as clearly as with words. Likewise, I could not sing any song that would give
me the out-of-this-material-world experience I get while playing Herbie Hancock's modal jazz masterpiece Maiden Voyage. I think I could write
a whole piece about the depth of impresionism that modal and ECM-style jazz (the music that never _goes_ anywere) can provide like no other style.
